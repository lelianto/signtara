Word-sign model (public/models/words-v1) — training data attribution
=====================================================================

The words-v1 model weights were trained on landmark sequences extracted from the
datasets below. The videos themselves are NOT redistributed with this application.

1. WL-BISINDO — Word-Level BISINDO video dataset (Banten variant)
   Kindy, Leonali, Lucky (BINUS University). "Word-Level BISINDO: A Novel Video
   Indonesian Sign Language Dataset and Baseline Methods", Procedia Computer Science,
   2025, doi:10.1016/j.procs.2025.08.277
   Data: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/glennleonali/wl-bisindo (Glenn Leonali)
   Code: https://github.com/AceKinnn/WL-BISINDO
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0)
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
   Because this model is trained on WL-BISINDO, the words-v1 weights are distributed
   under CC BY-NC 4.0 as well: non-commercial use only, with attribution.

2. BISINDO 40 Kata mp4 — Anggi Yohanes Pardede
   https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/anggiyohanespardede/bisindo-40-kata-mp4
   License: MIT (as declared on the dataset page)

3. Bahasa Isyarat Indonesia Dinamis — Muhammad Dhia Ulhaq
   https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/muhammaddhiaulhaq/bahasa-isyarat-indonesia-dinamis
   License: MIT (as declared on the dataset page)

Landmark models used for extraction and at runtime (Apache-2.0, Google MediaPipe):
   hand_landmarker.task, pose_landmarker_lite.task, face_landmarker.task
   https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision — see MediaPipe-Apache-2.0.txt

Signs in these datasets have not been reviewed by this project's Deaf community
reviewers. Regional BISINDO variants differ; a recognised word reflects the variant
of the recorded signers.
